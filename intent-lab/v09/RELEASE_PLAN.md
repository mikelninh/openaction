# V0.9 Release Plan · The Listening Bridge

**North star:** Transform a decorative interaction into a small playable musical exchange, without expanding world scope.

**Beats:** (1) rescue Mia with the publicly anchored thread; (2) give the rope a note; (3) see/hear a voluntary three-note response; (4) return its tune using low/middle/high positions; (5) watch the constellation awaken and keep playing.

**Cuts:** No sign-up, leaderboard, coins, new islands to explore, dashboards, automated spending, online collaboration, blockchain claims or fictional real-world proof.

**Acceptance:** first note changes the reply melody; no-sound visual alternative; real keyboard/touch/mouse completion; wrong notes never reset correct progress; repeatable call; responsive rope and audible tones; recorded simulated actions and tamper check; end scene remains playable; replay clears state.

**Verified:** desktop, mobile, narrow phone, reduced motion, sound, keyboard, accessible transcript, and forged-journal detection. These are automated browser tests, not independent player feedback.

**V1.0 discovery gate:** Recruit five test players; aim for at least four to complete the first 90–120 seconds without intervention and at least three to describe a memorable moment in their own words. If they struggle, improve clarity and physical feel before adding another chapter.
