# COMMONS · The Listening Bridge (V0.9)

A one-file playable micro-adventure about rescue, consent, and a three-note conversation across a handmade rope bridge.

**Play:** open [index.html](./index.html) in a modern browser. No account, wallet, API, tracking, network request, or audio permission is required. Sound is optional and starts off.

## The first 90–120 seconds

1. Drag the golden spool to the **shared willow**, not the **private doorway**. Brace it against the wind bell. Mia crosses.
2. Pluck anywhere on the bridge to offer a first note. The cottage composes a **three-note call based on that note**.
3. Listen with your eyes or ears, and return the tune by plucking **low / middle / high** along the same rope. Correct notes light up; mistakes never erase your previous progress.
4. The horizon opens when all three notes are right. You can still pluck the bridge afterwards.

**Keyboard:** Space to assist with initial connection; 1 / 2 / 3 for low, middle, high; R to replay the melody; J for field notes; Esc to close them. Phone users can tap the knots and three rope regions.

## Release gate

Browser-tested through the original rescue and the new duet at 1440×900, 390×844, 320×640, and with reduced motion. Tests also check sound on/off, keyboard-only play, visual melody cues, retries, replay, journal hashing, and post-finale freeplay. Full source, source-builder and test harness are also distributed in the V0.9 conversation release package.

## Important boundary

Everything here is **fictional and simulated**. The private route is blocked by game rules; the cottage's consent is a scripted story event, **not actual human consent**. Hashes make the local sequence tamper-evident but do not prove events independently. No AI agent, payments, Midnight or Moriarty smart contracts have been integrated.

**V1.0 gate:** Test the first two minutes with five people before adding content. Prioritise comprehension, delight and whether they want to replay; do not equate working code with product validation.
